Sources, coverage and calculation

All displayed values use the 2024 Journal Impact Factor reporting year. They describe the publication venues using a common-year snapshot, rather than the journal ranking when each article was published. Each bibliography entry is counted once in this overview; overlapping research collections are not added together.

The subtotal adds the journal’s 2024 impact factor once for each covered entry, so a journal is counted again for each publication in it. It is a descriptive sum of journal-level values, not an official researcher impact metric. Q3 and Q4 counts of zero refer only to the verified subset. Unverified entries are excluded, not treated as zero.
