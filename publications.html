Aggregate JIF

Aggregate JIF = eligible papers in a journal × that journal’s verified 2024 JIF. A paper contributes once, even if it appears in multiple research-area collections. For example, 7 eligible papers × 6.5 for Computers & Industrial Engineering = 45.5.

The displayed subtotal is 45.5 + 4.4 + 3.3 = 53.2, covering 10 of 52 eligible papers in 3 journals. Unknown values are omitted, never estimated or treated as zero. This is a descriptive sum of journal-level indicators, not article-level impact, a citation count or an official researcher evaluation metric.
