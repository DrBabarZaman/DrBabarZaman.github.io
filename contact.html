Helpful Enquiry Details

I welcome enquiries about research collaboration, postgraduate supervision, statistical consultancy, quantitative analysis for financial markets and invited lectures.

My expertise includes statistical process monitoring, adaptive and multivariate control charts, robust statistical methods, machine learning, biostatistics and clinical data analysis. My research interests also extend to physics-informed neural networks, digital twins and financial applications of statistical and machine-learning methods.

Please include your name, institution, relevant background and the purpose of your enquiry.

  • Research collaboration: Outline your research question, proposed methods and potential area of collaboration.
  • Postgraduate supervision: Share your CV and a brief description of your proposed topic, objectives and intended degree programme.
  • Statistical consultancy: Describe your dataset, analysis requirements, expected outcomes and timeline.
  • Financial markets and quantitative analysis: Specify the market or instruments of interest and your objectives, such as predictive modelling, trading-strategy evaluation, risk analysis or identifying potentially undervalued stocks.
  • Academic invitations: Specify the topic, audience, format and proposed dates.
