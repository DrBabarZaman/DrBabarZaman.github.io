Digital twins

Connecting physical systems with computational models.

OverviewApplicationsPublicationsWork in progress

The foundations

What is digital twins?

A digital twin is a computational representation of a physical asset or system that is updated with observations of its real-world counterpart. Combining measurements, simulation and analysis allows the model to reflect changing conditions and support monitoring, diagnosis and prediction.
