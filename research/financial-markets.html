AI and ML for quantitative analysis

Statistical research across forex, stocks and cryptocurrencies.

OverviewApplicationsPublications

The foundations

What is ai and ml for quantitative analysis?

Quantitative financial analysis uses mathematical, statistical and computational methods to study market data, evaluate strategies and examine risk. AI and machine-learning methods can identify relationships in historical observations; meaningful evaluation requires testing on later, unseen data and accounting for practical trading conditions.

My research

Research focus

My current research interests extend statistical and machine-learning methods to financial markets, including forex, stocks and cryptocurrencies. Areas of interest include predictive modelling, trading-strategy evaluation, risk analysis and the quantitative study of potentially undervalued stocks.
