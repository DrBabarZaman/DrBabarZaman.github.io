# Certificate review

All 20 explicitly named source files were readable directly, even though macOS denied listing their parent directory. Original source PDFs were reviewed with text extraction; nine learning certificates and three scanned documents were rendered and inspected. The two original embedded images in the MathWorks DOCX were also inspected.

## Supported learning entries
Nine course/specialization certificates, including Mastering Digital Twins, Machine Learning, Databases and SQL for Data Science with Python, Statistics for Data Science with Python, Using Python to Access Web Data, Applied Plotting, Introduction to Data Science in Python, Practical Data Science with MATLAB, and Mathematical Biostatistics Boot Camp 1. Two MathWorks attendance activities are separate: Deep Learning Overview for Signals and Time Series (22 September 2021) and Introduction to AI with MATLAB (31 January 2023).

## Important evidence distinctions
- The filename 03 Data Science Fundamentals with Python and SQL.pdf actually certifies Databases and SQL for Data Science with Python, completed 24 August 2025. It is not evidence of completing the full IBM specialization.
- Two supplied Michigan certificates document Introduction to Data Science in Python and Applied Plotting. They do not establish completion of the entire Applied Data Science with Python Specialization.
- The MATLAB specialization certificate does support Practical Data Science with MATLAB, completed 7 January 2023, and names MathWorks.
- The biography/CV-listed Applied Data Science with Python Specialization, Data Science Fundamentals with Python and SQL, and Machine Learning and Reinforcement Learning in Finance are retained without completion dates or resource buttons because no corresponding full-program certificates were supplied.
- The MathWorks DOCX contains the already supplied 2021 seminar certificate plus a separate 2023 AI webinar attendance certificate. The second embedded certificate image is published unchanged as PNG; no new certificate was authored.
- 07 Applied Plotting and 12 L9UCXBWSKY2J have the same credential identifier and completion date. They are one learning activity, not two. The combined All Certificates file also repeats Applied Plotting. Only one is displayed.
- Boot Camp 1 is supported by the dated 2012 statement of accomplishment. No credential verification URL is printed, so no Verify Credential button is shown.

## Credential links and documents
Credential URLs were transcribed from PDF links or visually checked printed identifiers. The online credential endpoint was not independently validated: browser retrieval returned cache-miss errors. No completion claims depend on an online response; they are grounded in the supplied documents. All individual PDF downloads are byte-for-byte copies. The combined certificate bundle and DOCX container are not published.

## Other certificates
Reviewer documents for Quality and Reliability Engineering International (2021 and 2023 service), Journal of Chemometrics (2024 service) and Stat (2023 service) appear under Academic Service, not as courses. No review totals are published. The Saudi Ophthalmology document is appreciation for a 2014 presentation, not a course. Two Wiley Top Cited Article awards concern the same paper and have recognition periods 2021–2022 and 2022–2023; neither is counted as a new publication or educational qualification. Article counts are unchanged.
