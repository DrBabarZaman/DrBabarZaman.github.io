Teaching Contexts

My teaching experience spans GIK Institute, the University of Hafr Al Batin, and Fatima Jinnah Women University. I have taught students across engineering, artificial intelligence, data science, computer science, cybersecurity, and medical sciences programmes.

My approach connects mathematical and statistical concepts with practical examples, computational tools, and applications relevant to each discipline.

Curriculum Development

I have contributed to curriculum development for BS Financial Technology and BS Mathematics and Data Analytics, connecting mathematical and statistical foundations with computational skills, interdisciplinary applications, and practical problem-solving.

My curriculum development interests span statistical inference, mathematical modelling, optimization, machine learning, and programming for data analysis. Areas aligned with my research and professional experience include:

Mathematics for Machine Learning

Linear algebra, calculus, probability, and numerical methods.

Statistical Learning and Predictive Analytics

Regression, classification, model validation, and uncertainty quantification.

Statistical Process Monitoring

Quality control, anomaly detection, and adaptive monitoring of industrial and engineering systems.

Financial Data Science

Time-series analysis, quantitative investment research, financial risk modelling, and algorithmic trading methods.

Biostatistics and Health Analytics

Study design, clinical data management, and analysis of health outcomes.

Reliable and Responsible AI

Interpretability, reproducibility, data ethics, and monitoring model performance.

I emphasize Python and MATLAB applications, real-world datasets, project-based assessment, and clearly defined learning outcomes to help students translate theory into rigorous, practical analysis.
